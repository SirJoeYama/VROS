CINEMA

a floating screen you control with your hands

    Pinch (tap)
    play / pause
    Fist + twist
    clockwise fast-forwards, counter-clockwise rewinds (one turn = one minute)
    Pinch with both hands
    move, scale and turn the screen
    Remote by your hands
    poke: prev · −10 s · play · +10 s · next · the bar to seek
    Peace sign, held 1 s
    left hand: undo · right hand: redo (keep holding to step again)
    Palms together (hold)
    close the app, back to home
    Palm toward your eyes (hold)
    show or hide this help in XR

    Desktop: click = play/pause · wheel = scrub · ←/→ ±10 s · space · N / P next / previous

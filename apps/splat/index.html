Pinch with both hands

Fist + twist

MEASURE, then pinch

Poke the panel
scenes, move / measure, flip and turn upside-down splats, size, passthrough, auto spin
Palm up, held 1 s

Two fists, held 1 s

Peace sign, held 1 s
left hand: undo · right hand: redo (where you were and how the splat sat)
Right thumbs down (hold)
close the app, back to home
Palm toward your eyes (hold)

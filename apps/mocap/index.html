MOCAP

record your head and hands, animate characters in Rigger

CALIBRATE
hold a T-pose for the countdown
RECORD
3 s countdown, then perform; STOP on the panel
LEGS
planted feet, or a library walk / jog / crouch
Timeline
poke to scrub; SET IN / SET OUT trim the take
In Rigger
takes show in 4 ANIMATE (human), marked ●

Recording needs the headset (Quest hand tracking or controllers). Desktop: click the panel to play back and trim takes · drag the timeline to scrub · Space play / pause · I / O set in / out · L live · V view · Delete · Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo, redo

Mannequin, skeleton and animations by Mesh2Motion (CC0).
